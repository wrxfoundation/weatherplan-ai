## 1. Aethir Edge: maker, regional distributors, Korea, and whether it is still sold in 2026

### Takeaway
Aethir Edge (a Qualcomm-powered edge device, launched April 2024) was sold through its own site and storefront plus a named list of regional distributors. One listed entry is **Mintgreen for South Korea**. In Asia, Future Trends covers SEA (exclusive in Malaysia and Singapore) and MEDINIS Group and Metaxhash cover wider Asia. I found no Korean-native distributor, no Korean press about Edge sales in Korea, and no dated evidence that Edge is still actively sold in 2026. Several third-party shops show it as sold out. As a sales channel for another device, the Edge distributor network looks dormant and mostly non-Korean.

## 3. Aethir ecosystem funding: Ecosystem Fund, Catalyst, partner grants (status and fit)

### Takeaway
Aethir runs a **$100M Ecosystem Fund** (launched in late 2024). Inside it sit the **Aethir Catalyst** grant and subsidy program (AI and gaming, $5k–$200k in ATH plus up to 35% compute subsidy) and several co-branded partner grants. The most relevant is the **WeatherXM × Aethir Ecosystem Grant (announced 2025-12-08)**, aimed at weather-AI builders. It offers up to $10k in Aethir compute credits plus up to $10k in WeatherXM milestone support, and it **requires using WeatherXM data**. This is the single most concrete Aethir door for an "AI + real-world weather data" project. It also has a structural catch: it is built around a competing weather-station network.

### Cited Findings
- $100M Aethir Ecosystem Fund for AI and gaming. Aethir Catalyst is described as a $20M grant program backed by the Aethir Foundation, distributing 336M ATH split equally between AI and gaming over the year. Grants are $5k–$200k worth of ATH (one-time), with compute subsidies of up to 35% of base service cost. It targets more than 100 projects, judged on innovation, growth potential, compute requirements and community impact. **(Launch in Oct 2024: stale. Sources disagree on dates.)** — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/aethir-launches-100m-fund-accelerating-ai-gaming-innovation); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/aethir-catalyst-will-invest-100m-in-ai-and-gaming-startups-using-decentralized-gpus/); [Aethir blog](https://blog.aethir.com/blog-posts/100m-aethir-ecosystem-fund-next-level-growth-for-ai-and-gaming-innovators); [Aethir Catalyst page](https://aethir.com/AethirCatalyst)
- Aethir's 2025 wrap-up says it onboarded 50+ projects through grants and subsidies from the $100M fund, including Catalyst. This is a company-reported aggregate. **[search summary]** — [Aethir 2025 Wrap-Up](https://aethir.com/blog-posts/aethirs-2025-wrap-up-decentralized-gpu-cloud-milestones)
- Named grantee batch 6 (April 2025), the "RWA and AI-agent" batch: AGIXBT, Upside OS, Zoo Finance, PinLink, UnifAI Network. None is an IoT or sensor-data project. **[search summary] (stale)** — [Aethir April 2025 Recap](https://ecosystem.aethir.com/blog-posts/aethirs-april-2025-recap)
- **WeatherXM × Aethir joint program**, announced 2025-12-08. It links WeatherXM's station network to the Aethir Ecosystem Fund as "a unified path for builders across both ecosystems to access compute, data, hardware, and technical support". Target sectors: climate, agriculture, energy, logistics, insurance, **trading**, public infrastructure. — [WeatherXM blog](https://blog.weatherxm.com/weatherxm-and-aethir-announce-joint-program-to-accelerate-weather-ai-through-aethirs-100-million-8e32d5a3145f?gi=d5eb698c854c); [BlockchainReporter](https://blockchainreporter.net/aethir-ecosystem-integrates-weatherxm-to-accelerate-weatherai-with)
- BuildersXM FAQ: up to $10k Aethir compute credits plus up to $10k WeatherXM milestone-based support, along with hardware, APIs, datasets, technical collaboration and amplification. WeatherXM's grants site states a hard requirement: "your project must use WeatherXM's data". **[search summary, unverified]** — [BuildersXM](https://builders.weatherxm.com/); [WeatherXM grants](https://grants.weatherxm.com/)
- Aethir's Ecosystem Fund page lists the "WeatherXM x Aethir Ecosystem Grant" as open for applications and gives a general per-project range of $5k–$500k. This conflicts with the $5k–$200k Catalyst figure and with the WeatherXM $10k+$10k structure. **[search summary, unverified]** — [aethir.com/ecosystem-fund](http://aethir.com/ecosystem-fund)
- Other co-branded Aethir grant tracks exist, such as Aethir × Polyhedra (high-impact AI builders) and the Aethir fund supporting the Avalanche Foundation's infraBUIDL(AI) program. This shows a pattern of partner-network grants. — [Aethir × Polyhedra](https://ecosystem.aethir.com/blog-posts/aethir-and-polyhedra-launch-grant-program-for-high-impact-ai-builders); [Aethir × Avalanche infraBUIDL(AI)](https://ecosystem.aethir.com/blog-posts/aethirs-100m-ecosystem-fund-supports-avalanche-foundations-infrabuidl-ai-program)
- Aethir also launched "Aethir RWA Capital" (GPU rewards to growth capital). The post claims 435,000+ GPUs in 93 countries and 200+ locations (self-reported). — [Aethir RWA Capital](https://ecosystem.aethir.com/blog-posts/introducing-aethir-rwa-capital-from-gpu-rewards-to-growth-capital)

### Inferences
- **Catalyst fit is weak to moderate.** It funds projects that consume Aethir GPU compute; the subsidy is a share of compute cost. A device project gets something only if it has a real GPU workload, for example training or serving a refined indoor and outdoor weather or air-quality model, or inference for the prediction-market data products mentioned in the context. Without that workload, an application amounts to asking for ATH and is likely low priority.
- **The WeatherXM × Aethir grant is the most on-theme door, with two problems.** (1) It requires WeatherXM data, so Wellbian would need a product that combines KWeather, Wellbian indoor data and WeatherXM data, for example an indoor/outdoor exposure model for a trading or insurance use case. (2) WeatherXM is effectively an adjacent competitor (outdoor weather DePIN). The grant is small, at most $20k in mixed credits and support. Its value would be signalling and an introduction to both teams, not the money.
- The grant's sector list includes "trading", which matches the inquiries from prediction-market platforms. This is the most defensible angle if Wellbian applies: refined hyperlocal weather and air data as a trading or settlement input, with GPU inference on Aethir.
- Funding is paid in ATH (Ethereum/Arbitrum-native). Wellbian's on-chain stack is XRPL. Accepting ATH grants creates token-handling and disclosure questions. These should be checked against `depin/content/site-canon-0910.md` and `depin/intel/playbook.md` before applying.

### Gaps
- Could not open aethir.com/AethirCatalyst or /ecosystem-fund to confirm 2026 status, the application form URL, or whether Catalyst continued past its first year (it was stated as distributing over "the next year" from Oct 2024).
- No list of Catalyst grantees in IoT, sensors or environmental data was found. Absence is not proof that none exist.
- No Aethir accelerator or incubator separate from the fund was found.

## 6. Strategic direction 2026 (ACCELERATE, enterprise) and what it means for a small device project

### Takeaway
On **2026-08-24 Aethir launched "ACCELERATE"**: its own GPU data-centre capacity, with access to 10 sites (US and Europe), up to 20 MW, built for NVIDIA B300/GB300 clusters of 64–256 nodes. It targets up to $700M in contracts by end-2026 and over $2B at full build-out (self-reported). This continues the move away from consumer and edge hardware (Edge, Checker Nodes) toward enterprise AI compute. For a small air-quality device project, Aethir is now a GPU vendor and grant source, not a hardware distribution partner.

### Inferences
- **Aethir is a weak target for distribution.** Its hardware channels (Edge distributors, Checker Node launchpads) are from 2024, built around token yield, and not Korean.
- **Moderate fit, only with a real compute story:** (a) a WeatherXM × Aethir or Catalyst grant if Wellbian has a GPU workload (weather or air model inference, data for prediction markets); (b) a panel or co-host slot at an Aethir deAI/DePIN side event in Asia, on the GEODNET/Nubila precedent.
- **Recommended angle wording:** "real-world sensor data that AI models need" plus Korean weather-data credibility (KWeather). Avoid "fellow DePIN", token or yield talk, and any XRPL-vs-Arbitrum framing.
- **Honest flag:** in an enterprise data-centre phase, Aethir's BD team cares about MW and contracts. A sub-$20k grant or a panel seat is the realistic ceiling. For Korean hardware resale, multi-brand DePIN shops are better targets: Asia-based Metaxhash or MEDINIS, or EU shops already mapped in the WeatherXM work.
