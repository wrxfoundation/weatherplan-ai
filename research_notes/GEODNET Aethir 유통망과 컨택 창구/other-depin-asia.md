## Q1. Bucket 1: environmental, weather and air-quality DePIN projects that could partner or co-distribute. Are they active, and are they in Asia?

### Takeaway
**Nubila Network** is the strongest Bucket 1 target. It is an active weather and environmental DePIN with outdoor stations, previously IoTeX ioID-integrated and partnered with Wingbits and Weather Underground. It complements an indoor AQ monitor, but its data-oracle positioning **overlaps Wellbian's XRPL weather-data network**, so approach it as a coopetition partner. **Ambios** (ex-Ambient/PlanetWatch, air quality, Solana) is the only live air-quality DePIN found, but its 2026 activity looks thin. **Silencio** (noise, phone-based, peaq) and **Aira Labs** (UK AQ wearable, early-stage) rank lower. None of the four is confirmed to be Asia-headquartered. No Korean, Japanese or Taiwanese air-quality DePIN was found.
