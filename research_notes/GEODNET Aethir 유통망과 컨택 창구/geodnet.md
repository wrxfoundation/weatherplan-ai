## Q1. Official GEODNET distributor list, Asia coverage, and the HYFIX → GEODNET Store change

### Takeaway
The official lists name about 7 to 9 distributors. Only one covers East Asia: **Village Island (Tokyo)**. Two are in India (HYFIX India, Hepta Networks). No distributor is listed for Korea, Taiwan, Southeast Asia or Australia. The HYFIX.AI store has been rebranded as the **GEODNET Store** (store.geodnet.com). HYFIX Spatial Intelligence, Inc. still runs the store as the official hardware distributor, fulfilment provider and support desk. That rebrand appears to date from roughly early 2026, not September 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Distributor lists (three official pages that partly disagree)**
- Official docs page "Certified GEODNET Base Station Vendors" lists Crypship.io under **Asia**, HYFIX India (India) and Village Island (**East Asia**), among others. **[search summary, unverified]** — [GEODNET Docs](https://docs.geodnet.com/geod-mining/certified-geodnet-base-station-vendors)
- The GEODNET Store "Network" page lists Village Island for East Asia, HYFIX India, and Crypship.io as **EU / Worldwide**. This contradicts the docs page, which puts Crypship under Asia. **[search summary, unverified]** — [GEODNET Store / Network](https://store.geodnet.com/pages/network)
- The legacy HYFIX distributor page adds **Hepta Networks (India)**, **Helium Deploy (worldwide)** and **Technostorex (Turkey)**. **Novyx.Tech** appears as worldwide on one of the lists. **[search summary, unverified]** — [hyfix.ai distributors page](https://hyfix.ai/pages/geodnet-station-distributors)
- The GEODNET Store "about" page says the store "was previously operated under HYFIX.AI and transitioned to a unified storefront" for GEODNET hardware, subscriptions and positioning. **[search summary, unverified]** — [About GEODNET Store (Formerly HYFIX.AI)](https://store.geodnet.com/pages/about-geodnet-store-formerly-hyfix-ai)
- A community newsletter describes the rebrand: the HYFIX Store becomes the GEODNET Store, hosted on the GEODNET site. HYFIX Spatial Intelligence, Inc. "continues to operate the store and serves as official hardware distributor, storefront operator, fulfillment and logistics provider, and customer support provider." The same post mentions a GEO-SWARM Kickstarter "planned for March 10th", which points to a publication date around Feb–Mar 2026. **[search summary, unverified; date inferred]** — [GEODNET Info Substack, "The Next Chapter for GEODNET Starts Now"](https://geodnetinfo.substack.com/p/the-next-chapter-for-geodnet-starts)
- Support routes at the store: order questions go to a published generic HYFIX "info" address, and subscription credentials are sent from a generic HYFIX "subscriptions" address. There is also an e-mail contact page. **[search summary, unverified]** — [GEODNET Store e-mail contact page](https://store.geodnet.com/pages/e-mail-contact)
- Other RTK and data resellers seen in results include Mapping Network (Canada) and Deep Sand Technology (GEODNET RTK for autosteer). They sell RTK service to agriculture and surveying users, not mining hardware. — [mappingnetwork.ca](https://mappingnetwork.ca/pages/geodnet); [Deep Sand Technology](https://www.deepsandtechnology.com/geodnet-rtk)
- Hexaspot, which Wellbian has already contacted, presents itself as an "official affiliate" of GEODNET. It does not sell GEODNET products directly but earns commission through affiliate links. **[search summary, unverified]** — [Hexaspot GEODNET page](https://hexaspot.com/pages/geodnet)
- A former reseller (HashCypher) says that "as of 2025, we are no longer offering these products for direct sale" and sends buyers to GEODNET/HYFIX. **[search summary, unverified]** — [HashCypher](https://www.hashcypher.com/geodnet-weather-station/)
- Internal study: Village Island is the only official East Asia distributor and there is no Korean distributor. **[internal, 2026-09-18]** — `depin/reports/geodnet-community-study-0918.html`

### Inferences
- The channel is consolidating toward GEODNET's own storefront run by HYFIX. Independent resellers are either leaving (HashCypher) or moving to affiliate-only models (Hexaspot). For Wellbian, a pure "add your device to our shop" pitch to GEODNET-orbit resellers has a smaller pool than it seems: realistically Village Island, Crypship, HeliumDeploy and the two Indian distributors.
- Wellbian has already contacted Crypship and HeliumDeploy, and both appear on GEODNET's lists. Crypship's "Asia" label in the docs is a useful hook: Wellbian can ask whether Crypship actually ships to or has partners in Asia.
- Korea is an open slot: there is no Korean GEODNET distributor, KRW listings exist (Q2), and a free-station push for Korea/Japan ran in 2025. That gives Wellbian (Seoul, KWeather) a credible "Korea channel" offer to make to GEODNET.

### Gaps
- I could not open any official list, so the exact current roster and whether the docs, store and hyfix pages are in sync remain unverified. An outreach person should check store.geodnet.com/pages/network in a normal browser.
- I could not confirm a September 2026 migration. The evidence suggests early 2026. Whether hyfix.ai URLs now redirect is unknown.
- I found no Taiwan, Southeast Asia or Australia distributor in any summary.
- Distributor margin or commission terms are not public.

## Q4. "GEODNET Weather Station": what it is, and whether GEODNET wants environmental sensors

### Takeaway
"GEODNET Weather Station" is a **legacy or marketing name for the GNSS base station**. It measures **space weather** (ionosphere and troposphere effects on GNSS signals), not ground-level weather or air. Some third-party shops claim it also tracks temperature and humidity, but this is unverified. GEODNET's own paper treats meteorological sensors as optional parts of the reference design. I found **no air-quality plans** and no environmental-sensor partnership.

### Cited Findings
- Third-party descriptions call the network roof-mounted "Space Weather" stations that track how solar wind affects satellite navigation signals, used to correct GNSS to centimetre level. **[search summary, unverified]** — [startups.one.gob.es](https://startups.one.gob.es/companies/geodnet)
- MobileCM Triple-Band: "Geodetic Grade GNSS base-station that acts as an RTK base-station and collects Space Weather data", under 2 W, USB-C. No temperature, humidity, pressure or wind sensors are mentioned. **[search summary, unverified]** — [GEODNET Store product](https://store.geodnet.com/products/mobilecm-triple-band-gnss-base-station)
- FreshMiners lists a "GEODNET Weather Station" at €699 and claims it tracks temperature and humidity. Infinity Modern lists it at $749 as a "roof-mounted Space Weather station". Both are reseller marketing, not specs. **[search summary, unverified]** — [FreshMiners](https://freshminers.com/shop/hardware/geodnet-weather-station/); [Infinity Modern](https://infinity-modern.com/product/geodnet-weather-station/)
- GEODNET's NAVIGATION (ION) paper says reference designs can include meteorological sensors (temperature, pressure, humidity). **(>12 mo; paper ~2023)** **[search summary, unverified]** — [NAVIGATION 70(4), navi.605](https://navi.ion.org/content/70/4/navi.605)
- The 2022 HYFIX release on the mobile base station using Quectel LC29H gives the hardware lineage. **(>12 mo)** — [Business Wire 2022-07-11](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220711005249/en/HYFIX.AI-Announces-Availability-of-its-Mobile-Centimeter-GEODNET-Base-Station-Using-Quectel%E2%80%99s-LC29H-Dual-Band-GNSS-Module)
- An environmental DePIN does exist elsewhere: **Ambios**, which uses air-quality sensors. It is not linked to GEODNET in any source. — [Beluga DePIN list](https://heybeluga.com/articles/top-depin-projects-2025/)

### Inferences
- "Weather station" branding does **not** show that GEODNET wants environmental sensors on its network today. There is only a weak hook: the original architecture contemplated met sensors, and the network already describes itself as "Earth observation". Wellbian can use that hook ("complete the Earth-observation stack indoors") but should not claim GEODNET has an environmental-sensing program.
- There is overlap at the KWeather level: tropospheric delay from GNSS is a known input to meteorology (precipitable water vapor). A KWeather × GEODNET data conversation may be more natural than a device-bundle conversation. This is speculative and not sourced to GEODNET.

### Gaps
- No official spec sheet confirming any temperature or humidity sensor on GEODNET hardware.
- No statement from GEODNET or HYFIX on air-quality or environmental-sensor plans.

## Q6. Fit and risk signals (reward messaging, distributor economics, failures)

### Takeaway
Fit: GEODNET has reward-cut experience, B2B revenue, Korean listings and no Korean channel. Risk: the reseller layer is thin and consolidating, distributors historically did not hold stock, and one community-run distributor went bankrupt. That is a clear warning to keep customer money away from intermediaries in any Wellbian distributor deal.

### Cited Findings
- 50% reward halving took effect on 2025-07-01. **(>12 mo)** — [GEODNET on X](https://x.com/GEODNET_/status/1939866551068262491)
- Performance-based rewards (GIP-6, effective 2025-07-02): below-standard stations lose rewards, and a station 7 days offline drops from its hex's active count. **(>12 mo)** — [GEODNET Docs, performance rules](https://docs.geodnet.com/performance-based-reward-rules)
- Station count kept growing after the cuts: 20,500+ (Q3 2025) to 21,000+ (2026). — [Messari Q3 2025](https://messari.io/report/state-of-geodnet-q3-2025); [Blockchain Association](https://theblockchainassociation.org/posts/blockchain-association-member-spotlight-geodnet)
- Free-shipping promotion and the 20,000+ stations milestone were announced on 2025-11-28. **[search summary, unverified]** — [Messari project page](https://messari.io/project/geodnet)
- RTK pricing to end users: $400/year or $40/month. — [Geo-matching](https://geo-matching.com/products/geodnet)
- Distributors did not buy inventory: customers paid first and the distributor then ordered from the manufacturer. A distributor set up by a community leader went bankrupt. Moderators doubling as sellers was flagged as a risk. **[internal]** — `depin/reports/geodnet-community-study-0918.html` (digest in `research_notes/XRPL 인플루언서와 DePIN 고객 확보/depin_device_gtm_cases.md`)
- Founder said "no PR firm". An external-influencer FUD episode occurred in Oct 2023. **[internal]** — same study §07.
- Hexaspot's affiliate-only model means it earns commission and holds no stock. **[search summary, unverified]** — [Hexaspot](https://hexaspot.com/pages/geodnet)
- HashCypher exited direct sale in 2025. **[search summary, unverified]** — [HashCypher](https://www.hashcypher.com/geodnet-weather-station/)

### Inferences
- **Ranked outreach list (by fit):**
  1. **Village Island (Tokyo)**: the only East Asia distributor, already multi-DePIN (GEODNET + Wingbits), and runs its own store. Route: LinkedIn company page / Michael Van Dorpe (CEO), the generic sales address on its site, ATxSG Singapore (May).
  2. **GEODNET Foundation / HYFIX (partnership)**: pitch a "Korea ground partner and weather-data collaboration", not device distribution. Route: LinkedIn company page, X @GEODNET_, Mike Horton (CEO) by name, the GEODNET Store e-mail contact page for HYFIX hardware and channel questions.
  3. **Crypship.io**: already contacted. Follow up on its "Asia" listing in GEODNET docs.
  4. **HeliumDeploy**: already contacted. Worldwide GEODNET distributor; low Asia relevance.
  5. **HYFIX India / Hepta Networks (India)**: GEODNET distributors in India; only relevant if Wellbian targets India.
  6. **Hexaspot**: already contacted. Affiliate model, so an affiliate-link arrangement is the realistic ask.
- **Contract hygiene:** given the bankrupt-distributor precedent and the pay-first model, any Wellbian reseller deal should send customer payment to Wellbian or the store directly, and keep community moderators separate from sales.

### Gaps
- Distributor margins and commission rates are not public.
- I found no public data on distributor sales share or regional station counts for Korea or Japan.
