## 1. Master operator table: who operates, under what licence, with which weather topics, and whom to contact

### Takeaway
Only **one regulated venue outside the US explicitly permits climate contracts: Wealthsimple Predict in Canada.** It is built on Kalshi, and CIRO (Canada's investment-industry regulator) requires settlement periods of **at least 30 days**.

The other channels that carry weather are global:
- **Kalshi**, directly to users.
- **Interactive Brokers' Forecast Contracts**: ForecastEx climate contracts, sold to eligible clients of IB Ireland, IB Canada, IB Hong Kong and IB Singapore.
- **Polymarket**: daily temperature markets for Asian cities. This is the most sensitive counterpart for a Korean company.

UK exchanges run PM products under Gambling Commission (GC) licences but list no weather markets. These are Betfair Predicts, Matchbook (which hosts the ADI Predictstreet and easyBet brands) and Smarkets.

Named decision-makers were found only for Kalshi, Polymarket, ForecastEx, Smarkets and Opinion. For the other operators the people column is a gap.
