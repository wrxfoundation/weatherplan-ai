## 1. Master table: every entity at a glance

### Takeaway
Three kinds of paid role exist around weather contracts.

1. **Named settlement publishers:**
   - The Weather Company (TWC) for Kalshi. TWC is now listed **first**, ahead of the NWS, in the Source Agency hierarchy of Kalshi's generic temperature rulebook (2026-10-01 copy).
   - **Reask** for ForecastEx's "Live Hurricane" contracts. This is new, reported around 2026-10-08.
   - Speedwell/Xweather for CME.
   - Weather Underground (WU), owned by TWC (bg), is Polymarket's free display source outside the US.
2. **Liquidity providers:**
   - Susquehanna (SIG), with an 80-person team.
   - Jump, DRW, Wintermute and Galaxy.
   - Designated liquidity providers that Kalshi pays to quote **"Hourly Weather"**.
3. **Hedgers and structurers:** Arbol, Descartes, Discrete, and Cantor's block-trade clients.

The highest-value contacts for Wellbian are:
- **TWC's settlement-methodology owner (James Belanger)** and **Xweather's weather-risk head (David Whitehead)**, as verification-feed or reseller partners.
- **Reask**, as proof that a regulated venue will settle on a modelled value built from many points rather than one station.
- **SIG's business-development head (Joe Grubb)**, because SIG is the market maker embedded in the most venues.
- **Arbol**, the only weather-derivatives firm in this set with a confirmed New York HQ for Oct 24–29.

### Inferences
- **TWC is both gatekeeper and buyer.** TWC is first-ranked even for non-US `<area>`, and its US values simply mirror the NWS. So its value-add is accountability, not new measurement. Outside the US it will need either national met-service feeds or a verified consensus. That makes **TWC the most important reseller target and the most dangerous competitor** at once. Pitch "neighbour-checked, ledger-timestamped values and anomaly flags for non-US cities, delivered to TWC", not a replacement for TWC at Kalshi.
- **Reask lends credibility to the multi-feed approach.** Reask shows a regulated venue will settle on a modelled, multi-point value with a published method, and will publish the vendor's forecast as on-venue fair value. That supports the design of Wellbian's hourly consensus plus met / not met / withheld verdicts. Reask's own argument (stations go dark in storms) parallels the April 2026 Paris CDG sensor incident.
- **Xweather already runs the target business.** Its settlement services sell certified, versioned weather data to an exchange. A data-supply or co-branding deal for Asian cities is the most natural business shape (inference). The weak point: buyers here will ask where the eleven feeds come from. Internally, 8 of 11 come via Open-Meteo, so "independent" will be challenged, and redistribution rights must be confirmed before any resale. — [internal record](./internal_status.md)
- **A licensing gap may exist.** Synoptic's exclusion clause suggests that observation aggregators without a trading licence leave settlement-grade and trading-grade feeds unserved. Wellbian should check that none of its upstream feeds carries a similar prohibition.
- **Match station precision to each venue.** Kalshi resolves at full source precision ("exactly" means one decimal place), while Polymarket's WU-based Seoul market uses whole °C. Wellbian's per-city ICAO reference station plus consensus must document how it maps to each venue's precision and rounding.

### Inferences
- **What a market maker would buy** (inference from the adverse-selection and hourly-weather evidence): faster, cleaner intraday observations and early warning of station anomalies. A Paris CDG-type spike is exactly the event that picks off market makers. Wellbian's hourly consensus and anomaly flags fit this need.
- **Hard line on settlement values.** If Wellbian's values are, or may become, a settlement input anywhere, market makers must not receive verdicts or settlement-relevant values before the public. That is the "know it before others" problem the CFTC named.
  - Workable split (inference): (a) settlement verdicts (met / not met / withheld) are published to the public ledger first, with no paid early access; (b) any commercial feed to market makers carries observation data under equal-latency, documented terms.
  - Wellbian also needs a written conflict policy, since a settlement-data vendor selling to the largest liquidity providers will be asked about it.
- **SIG first, but sequence it.** SIG is the only market maker touching Kalshi, Rothera, Cantor and OTC. Its business-development head (Joe Grubb) is the right door; Maletz and Best are seniority options.
  - Internal sequencing matters: Rothera/Robinhood contacts are gated on the 10/15 Crypto.com decision.
  - Citadel Securities is a Crypto.com/OG.com investor, and the mutual NDA with Crypto.com may bind; do not name OG.com.
- **Polymarket-side market makers carry Korean risk.** Jump and Wintermute quote Polymarket, which lists Asian city weather including Seoul (Incheon). Data offered to them must not touch Seoul until counsel advises (internal rule).
