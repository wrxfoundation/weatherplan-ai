## Part A — Which chain-native prediction markets exist on XRPL, the XRPL EVM sidechain and Flare (plus RLUSD-denominated ones), and which programs surface their builders?

### Takeaway
I found no live prediction market with verifiable traction on XRPL, the XRPL EVM sidechain or Flare.
- **Axiom** (XRPL EVM, trading in XRP/RLUSD) had a beta announced for 2026-01-19, and nothing newer turned up.
- **FlarePredict** looks test-stage (Coston2 token).
- **The rest are hackathon prototypes.**

The realistic routes to builders are:
- the **XRP Ledger Hackathon NYC (Oct 24–25, hosted by XRPL Commons)** and the **Swell + Apex week (Oct 26–29)**;
- **Flare's own developer hub**, whose reference "weather insurance" app settles on **OpenWeatherMap** data. That is a concrete swap-in opportunity.

Flare has **deprecated FDC Web2Json on all networks** (no mainnet source). Web2 weather data on Flare now goes through **FCC (TEE) extensions** or a new FIP.14 proposal.

## Part B — Which oracle / settlement networks resolve prediction markets or carry weather data, and how does an outside data company become a provider?

### Takeaway
In the official docs and catalogs I could read (Chainlink DataLink, Pyth, Flare FTSO, API3, Supra, DIA, Kleros), **no weather feed appears**. Weather is whitespace. Chainlink's only weather precedent found is AccuWeather's own node (2021, current status unknown). On Flare, weather appears only in reference apps (built on OpenWeatherMap) and in KWeather's own contracts [I].
- **Chainlink.** The only published intake that fits a non-financial data company is **Chainlink DataLink** ("Interested in being a data provider for DataLink? Contact Chainlink Labs"). Its catalog today is purely financial: Deutsche Börse, FTSE Russell, S&P Global, Tradeweb, NCFX.
- **Pyth.** Explicitly limits publishers to "exchanges, market makers, and trading firms".
- **Flare.** "Data provider" means running FTSO/FDC infrastructure. Web2 data now enters through FCC extensions, or a FIP.14 proposal for a mainnet Web2 source.
- **UMA.** On Polymarket's managed oracle, **the proposer whitelist is controlled by Polymarket** (2/2 multisig with Risk Labs), so there is no "data provider" role.
- **Kleros/Reality.eth.** Crowd/juror oracles with no data-provider role.
- **API3.** Its first-party model fits a weather API company conceptually, but there is no visible intake.
- **RedStone, Switchboard, Band and Chronicle** could not be verified this session.
