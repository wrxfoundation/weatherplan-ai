## 1. Master table and what changed since 2026-09-20

### Takeaway
Of the 30 venues and groups covered, only three state any weather or climate intent:
- **Xchange Alpha**, a DCM designated 2026-01-30. Its site advertises "Forecast Contracts" on climate events, but no launch has been confirmed.
- **Fanatics Markets**, which is buying its own DCM and DCO from BGC. "Climate"/weather is on its Phase-Two roadmap.
- **Limitless Markets US**, a pending DCM. It is already in an inbound conversation with us about weather markets [INT].

The sports-led venues have no weather markets (DraftKings DKeX, Novig, ProphetX, Underdog, Sporttrade, JuiceX). ProphetX is the clearest example of a sports venue moving into non-sports categories through self-certification.

The biggest changes in the window:
- Blockchain.com applied for a DCM licence (2026-10-09).
- A CFTC proposal (2026-10-09) would put weather-linked event contracts explicitly inside the swap definition.
- ProphetX filed exchange-volume contracts (Oct 6–7).
- Novig was reportedly valued at $2B (Sept 30).
- MGM and Caesars said they are staying out (G2E, Sept 29–30).
- The IG Group purchase of Underdog and the Fanatics purchase of a DCM/DCO, both announced in late July, are still pending.

### Inferences
- **Reconciling CNBC's counts.**

  - Five of the six 2026 approvals can be identified: Xchange Alpha, ProphetX, Ludlow/Novig, Optex and JuiceX. The sixth is unidentified.
- **The 2026-10-09 swap-definition proposal names weather explicitly.** If finalized, weather event contracts would sit expressly inside CFTC swap jurisdiction. That supports the federal, non-gaming status sports contracts are fighting for in court, and lowers the regulatory risk for any venue that adds weather. Sports-led venues face state pressure: the NY AG petition, and MGM and Caesars staying out. That gives them a growing reason to add non-sports categories, and ProphetX's political and volume contracts show this is already happening.
- **The best timing lever is the rulebook and launch stage.** Several venues are standing up new contract lists now: Fanatics' newly acquired DCM, Xchange Alpha, JuiceX, 365Prediction, Smarkets and Blockchain.com. A settlement source written into contract terms at launch is stickier than a later swap.
