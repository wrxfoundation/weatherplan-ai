## 1. Program inventory: operator, launch date, status, size, regional chapters (Korea and Japan)

### Takeaway
The 2025–26 programs that are clearly active and funded all follow a similar pattern: **chapters + cohorts + grants**. Examples are Avalanche Team1, Solana Superteam, Stellar's new four-tier program (Aug 2026), the Arbitrum Ambassador Program, the Aptos Collective, Enjin Envoys (Jul 2026), and Akash's quarterly-funded roles. Several 2020–22-era programs (Polygon Advocates, The Graph Advocates, Chainlink, NEAR hubs) have little dated public evidence after 2023. Polkadot's program is in a contested restructuring.

On-record presence in Korea is concentrated in programs that have paid local leads or chapters: Superteam Korea, Arbitrum's Korea track, Base's South Korea country lead, a TON Society hub, and the 2022 NEAR Korea Hub. In Japan, the only dedicated chapter found is Superteam Japan.

  - numbered community calls, e.g. "Community Call #5 – WarmUp to Win at Colosseum", which prepares people for a hackathon. **(E)** [Luma](https://luma.com/l3nxt4m6?locale=ko)

## 2. Structure: tiers and levels, application and selection, term length, promotion and removal

### Takeaway
Most programs use a **3–4 step ladder**: an open or learning entry tier, then a "contributor" tier earned through proof of work, then a **small, selective upper tier** with stipends or leadership roles. Promotion is earned through sustained output over time (Enjin), seasonal rank-up applications (Nym), or delivered artifacts (Stellar).

Term length is usually defined indirectly, through **cohorts or seasons**: Aptos cohorts, Nym seasons, Chainlink application windows, Polkadot cycles, and Akash quarterly funding. Removal rules are rarely published. The exceptions are Polygon's 90-day activity minimum, Polkadot's referendum-based Head Ambassador removal, and NEAR's "asked to leave" handbook page.

### Inferences
- **Acceptance rates (derived):**
  - Aptos Summer 2025: 39 of 8,000+, about **0.5%**.
  - Aptos Fall 2025: 41 of 2,000+, about **2%**.
  - Arbitrum Phase 1: 160 of 3,000+, about **5%**.
  - Foundations publicize large applicant pools and very small accepted cohorts. A small DePIN project should expect a far smaller pool and can use a cohort of roughly 10–40.
- **Slot caps are the common lever** for controlling cost and quality in paid tiers: Enjin 10 + 5, Superteam's historical 150, and Aptos's cohorts of about 40.
- **Promotion criteria are mostly about frequency and duration** (Enjin: weekly for "a couple of months") or concrete artifacts (Stellar: deployed contract or MVP; Superteam: bounty or grant wins), not follower counts.
- **Term limits are implied by seasons and cohorts.** No program reviewed publishes a fixed term (e.g., "12 months"). Re-application or seasonal rank-up appears to stand in for term renewal.

## 3. Rewards by type: fixed vs performance-based

### Takeaway
Fixed monthly token stipends are **rare and confined to a few capped top slots**: Enjin Vanguard and Legend, and Nym's graded ambassadors. Polkadot tried salaries and met resistance.

The broad base of ambassadors gets **non-cash perks**: swag, Discord roles, badge NFTs, early access, core-team access, education, and speaking slots. On top of that, many programs offer **reimbursed event budgets** and **milestone-gated grants**: Stellar Instawards up to $15k, Team1 grants up to $10k/$30k, Graph AdvocatesDAO grants up to $20k, and Base Builder Grants. Performance-linked cash appears as bounties, conversion-based referral rewards (Nym: NYM per paid subscription), and points that unlock perks (Filecoin Orbit).

- **Arbitrum (adjacent program, not ambassadors).** The "Thank ARB" program paid **Tier 1 reviewers 100 ARB per application review** and **Tier 2 reviewers 10 ARB per review** for "initial assessments to filter out spam and ineligible projects". **(P)** [Arbitrum forum](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/thank-arb-program-update-assessors-needed/24511)

- **Polygon.** "Exclusive access and work with the core Polygon Labs team, meet industry leaders". **(P)** [polygon.technology/advocate-program](https://polygon.technology/advocate-program)
- **Paid career pathways.** Base staffs paid **Country Lead** roles. **(S)** [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/356981/base-layer-2-accelerates-global-expansion-with-seven-new-country-leads). TON Society hires paid **Hub Leads**. **(J)** [web3.career](https://web3.career/i/=YTO5cDO). These are the clearest "ambassador → job" ladders found.

### Inferences
- **Enjin's maximum monthly ENJ outlay (derived)** is 10 × 2,000 + 5 × 7,000 = **55,000 ENJ per month**, if all slots are filled, plus Beam credits. Capping slots makes the stipend budget predictable whatever the token price.
- **Budget in USD, pay in token** (Akash). The implied AKT price was about **$2.57** in Q1 2024, **$3.29** in Q4 2024 and **$1.19** in Q2 2025 (derived from the USD and AKT pairs above). The Q2 2025 request needed about **3.4× more AKT for about 1.2× more USD** than Q4 2024. Programs that pay in their own token carry this price risk, and governance has to re-approve token amounts each period.
- **Polkadot's arithmetic (derived).** The 1.98M USDC request is consistent with **about 9 months × $220k per month**, the monthly figure reported for the Ascent request. Large multi-month salary bundles drew the strongest pushback (see Section 6).
- **Fixed vs performance pattern:**
  - **Fixed:** small, capped top-tier stipends (Enjin, Nym), plus standing perks for all members (swag, roles, access).
  - **Performance or milestone-based:** grants (Stellar, Team1), bounties (Superteam, Team1, Akash), referral conversions (Nym), and points-to-perks (Orbit).
  - **Reimbursement-based:** event budgets (Orbit, Arbitrum, Aptos, Team1, The Graph). This is the lowest-risk cash reward, because money only follows a delivered event with itemized costs.

### Gaps
- **Missing reward amounts:** Sui, Aptos Collective, Chainlink, Polygon and Team1 member perks (beyond grants), Stellar Instawards per-chapter budgets, and Cardano Phase 2 ADA amounts.
- **Vesting, KYC and tax handling:** not captured for any program. Stellar's and Avalanche's terms pages exist but could not be read.
- **Nym:** the pay currency and date of the grade pay table.

## 4. Activities: what ambassadors are asked to do (required vs optional)

### Takeaway
Activities fall into a common menu: **local events and meetups, content, education and workshops (often at universities), translation, community moderation and support, newcomer onboarding, and builder or developer support**. Hard requirements are few and **frequency-based**:
- Sui: one event per month, plus a Discord or Telegram presence.
- Polygon: at least one activity every 90 days.
- Enjin: posting cadence per tier.
- Polkadot: full-time-level activity for Head Ambassadors.

Everything else is an optional menu. Specialized sub-roles (Akash's support, onboarding, content and meetup roles; Cardano's four roles) split the menu into accountable lanes.

### Inferences
- **Published requirements are minimal and measurable**: an event count per month, an activity per 90 days, or a posting cadence. Programs avoid long mandatory checklists, which suits volunteers.
- **Closest analogs for a device-based DePIN:**
  - **Akash's Vanguards and Navigators**, a support-first role with an explicit target for response time or share of questions answered. Device owners need setup and troubleshooting help more than they need hype content.
  - **Filecoin's event-in-a-box + reimbursement** for local events.
  - **NEAR Korea Hub's and Team1's university-club channel**, for Korean campus outreach.
- **Translation and localization is a standing role** in Cardano, Orbit, Enjin and The Graph. It is directly useful for a Korea-first project localizing into English, and the reverse.

### Gaps
- **Reporting forms and cadence:** the exact cadence (monthly report? per-event report?) was not captured for most programs. Only seasonal reports (Nym), quarterly funding proposals (Akash), weekly hangouts and monthly AMAs (Aptos), and recurring calls and all-hands (Superteam Korea, Team1 Vietnam, Orbit) were seen.
- **Disclosure rules for sponsored or ambassador posts** (e.g. "#ad" or "#ambassador" requirements): not captured. The T&C pages were unreadable.

## 5. Measurement and tooling: KPIs, scoring and points systems, reporting, tools

### Takeaway
Publicly reported KPIs are mostly **activity and funnel counts**: events per month, number of workshops, universities reached, content pieces, social reach, Luma subscribers, and applications vs acceptances. **Outcome metrics are rarer**: Akash's share of Discord questions answered, Filecoin's projects deployed, and Superteam's demo teams that raised funding.

Points and seasons appear in Nym (Mission Board and seasons), Filecoin Orbit (points unlock perks) and TON (achievement badges). Common tools are **Luma** (events and subscriber counts), **Discord** (roles and onboarding), **Notion** (handbooks and mission boards), **forum and on-chain proposals** (budgets and promotions), **Devfolio** (hackathons), and bounty boards (Superteam Earn, Avalanche's former "Snowfort").

X-based post-to-earn tracking became fragile after **X banned apps that reward users for posting (15–16 Jan 2026)**.

## 6. Lessons: documented results, shutdowns and redesigns, reward farming, low-quality content, compliance

### Takeaway
The best-documented failures are about **selection and money, not content**. Polkadot's ambassador collective faced charges of nepotism and favoritism, oversized and bundled salary budgets, and a failed **1.98M USDC** referendum. Its fix was smaller proposals, open roles and transparent selection. Broad, loosely defined reward criteria produced **uneven, low-creativity submissions** (Flamingo); the platform-level answer to farming arrived when **X banned reward-for-posting apps (Jan 2026)**.

Redesigns follow a clear pattern:
- concentrate on fewer, proven contributors: Aptos's refresh and Vanguard tier, Enjin's slot caps
- focus on a few regions before expanding: Arbitrum went from 36 countries in Phase 1 to 3 in Phase 2, then 5 and more
- add milestone-gated grants: Team1 in 2026
- move budgets on-chain or into the open: Akash quarterly, Polkadot OpenGov
- rebrand when a program hands over to a new structure: Avalanche Ambassador DAO → Team1; Web3 Foundation program → on-chain Fellowship

### Cited Findings
- **Polkadot: governance and selection lessons**
  - **Criticisms when moving on-chain (2024):** dependence on "regional" ambassadors created "nepotistic relations in some regions"; "some very active ecosystem members were not ambassadors"; and "incentives would be misaligned if people started getting paid within the existing structure". **(P)** [Forum: Lessons learned from the reform of the Ambassador Program](https://forum.polkadot.network/t/lessons-learned-from-the-reform-of-the-ambassador-program/9606)
  - **"Project Phoenix":** elected Head Ambassadors proposed a "fundamental reform", aiming to make the program "dynamic, inclusive, resilient, equitable, clear, and trustless". **(P)** [Forum: Project Phoenix](https://forum.polkadot.network/t/project-phoenix-the-fundamental-reform-of-the-polkadot-ambassador-programme/10395)
  - **First funding request:** Ref 1287 **passed for $640,000**, and the Fellowship **returned $230,000**. **(P)** [Polkassembly Ref 1287](https://polkadot.polkassembly.io/referenda/1287); context in [Ref 1614](https://polkadot.polkassembly.io/referenda/1614)
  - **Second request: Ref 1614 failed.** It asked for **1.98M USDC**.
    - **Objections:** "salaries were seen as too high and not well-justified, with an imbalance between leadership and grassroots contributors"; "unclear selection process for roles with perception of favoritism or nepotism"; "too large and bundled, mixing unrelated initiatives"; "doubts about program effectiveness… or return on investment".
    - **Promised changes:** break the proposal into smaller parts, make roles openly accessible with transparent selection, and rebalance the budget toward active community members.
    - **(P)** [Polkassembly Ref 1614](https://polkadot.polkassembly.io/referenda/1614)
  - **Earlier retracted attempt:** "Vote Nay – retracting following feedback – will resubmit under different terms". **(P)** [Subsquare Ref 1607](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1607)
  - **Guardrails written into the Ascent request:** "the community can put in a referendum to stop the funding at any point", and ambassadors can "pull a trigger clause to pause or stop funding for any of the projects". **(P)** [Forum: Transition to the On-Chain Collective](https://forum.polkadot.network/t/transition-to-the-on-chain-collective-next-steps-for-the-ambassador-fellowship/13686)
  - **Advisory Board statement:** the Fellowship's Advisory Board issued an official statement during the transition; its content n/c. **(P)** [Forum](https://forum.polkadot.network/t/official-statement-of-the-advisory-board-of-the-polkadot-ambassador-fellowship/12810/10)
- **Avalanche: rebrand and scale-up.** The Ambassador DAO, which ran its own bounty platform (Snowfort), was rebranded to Team1 (Snow Reports, Oct/Dec 2024). By 2026 it reported 450+ members, 600+ events and 130 universities, and it added a milestone-based grants layer on 1 Jul 2026. The sources captured do not state the *reasons* for the rebrand. **(P)** [Snow Report Oct 2024](https://info.avax.network/avalanche-snow-report-october-2024); [Avalanche blog 2026](https://www.avax.network/about/blog/avalanche-team1-applications-are-now-open); **(S)** [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/avalanche-team1-builder-grants-program/)
- **Arbitrum: focus, then expand**
  - **Phase 1 (2023):** a broad, student-focused cohort of 160 across 36 countries.
  - **Phase 2 (Aug 2024):** narrowed to **3 countries** (Nigeria, India, Mexico) and reported about 1,200 content pieces and 122M reach.
  - **2025:** added Korea and Brazil, then Singapore, Indonesia and Argentina, then Greater China and the Philippines.
  - **(P)** [Phase 1](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/introducing-the-arbitrum-ambassador-program-phase-1/); [Phase 2](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/arbitrum-ambassador-program-phase-2-kicks-off-in-nigeria-india-mexico/); [Expansion](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/arbitrum-ambassador-program-expands-to-new-regions/); [Greater China and the Philippines](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/greater-china-and-the-philippines-join-the-arbitrum-ambassador-program-expansion/)
  - The Foundation's yearly reporting covers the program. **(P)** [Arbitrum Foundation 2024 Transparency Report](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/the-arbitrum-foundation-2024-transparency-report-a-year-of-key-milestones-and-progress/28832)
- **Aptos: refresh toward top contributors.** The refreshed program focuses "on members who are actively involved… to keep the Collective concentrated on top contributors to provide stronger resources, more recognition, and bigger impact", with a path to a "Vanguard" tier. **(P)** [Forum: Aptos Collective Refresh](https://forum.aptosfoundation.org/t/aptos-collective-refresh/15797)

### Inferences
- **Selection transparency is the most important design choice** for a small project. Polkadot shows that paying people inside an opaque, regionally gatekept structure creates nepotism claims and budget rejection. A public rubric, open calls, and the ability for very active non-members to enter all reduce this risk.
- **Keep paid tiers small and budgets unbundled.** Polkadot's large bundled salary request failed. Akash, by contrast, filed small, line-itemed, USD-denominated requests every quarter (2023–Q2 2025); their pass or fail outcomes were not captured. Short funding periods with itemized lines and stop clauses look like the defensible pattern.
- **Narrow, outcome-specific criteria beat broad "any content counts" criteria** (the Flamingo lesson). After January 2026, rewarding volume of X posts is both a quality risk and a platform-policy risk.
- **Pilot narrowly, then expand.** Stellar ran a West Africa pilot in May 2024 before its global four-tier launch in Aug 2026. Arbitrum used 3 countries before adding Korea and others. For a Korean DePIN, this suggests a single-city or single-campus pilot before multi-region chapters.
- **Volunteer-only programs depend on outside grant pipelines.** Cardano's reliance on Catalyst left ambassadors exposed when Catalyst paused. A small project should budget event reimbursements directly rather than depend on uncertain third-party grants.
- **Expect farming once rewards are public.** Ambassador programs are listed on drop-hunting aggregators (CryptoRank pages for Stellar and Enjin). Slot caps, cadence-over-time thresholds (Enjin) and human review (Arbitrum's paid reviewer tiers in Thank ARB) are the observed defences.

### Gaps
- **Official reasons for redesigns** were not captured for: Avalanche's ADAO → Team1 rebrand, Filecoin Orbit's relaunch, Aptos's refresh, and Enjin's move from the older "Envoy" program to the July 2026 Envoys.
- **Shutdowns:** no documented closure of a major L1/L2 ambassador program was found in 2025–26. Polygon Advocates, The Graph Advocates and the NEAR hubs might be dormant, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Compliance problems:** no documented cases (securities or advertising-disclosure enforcement, KYC failures, ambassador misconduct) were found for the programs in scope. T&C pages were unreadable, and the search budget ran out before targeted searches. This is likely covered by the parallel "rules/risks" research stream.
- **Abuse statistics** (e.g. share of submissions rejected as spam or sybil): none published in the captured sources.
