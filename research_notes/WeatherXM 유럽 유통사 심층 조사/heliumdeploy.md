# HeliumDeploy (heliumdeploy.com): reseller profile for a Wellbian Labs approach

> **Method note (read first).** Research date: 2026-10-10. The agent proxy blocked heliumdeploy.com, trustpilot.com and manuallyreviewed.com directly (CONNECT 403 / DNS failure), so **no page was read in full**. Every finding below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets of the cited URLs. Treat all of it as **UNVERIFIED snippet-level evidence** until someone opens the pages in a normal browser. Company self-claims are labelled "(self-claim)". Personal emails and phone numbers were deliberately not recorded. Aggregator pages such as ZoomInfo and RocketReach were used only for names and roles.

## 1. Legal entity, founders/executives, founding year, team size, HQ

### Takeaway
HeliumDeploy is a Quebec-based e-commerce reseller. Its ToS is governed by Quebec law with Quebec City courts, and most profiles place it in Montreal/Westmount. The publicly named co-founders are Emile Chouha (co-founder since 2021) and Georges Harfouche (listed as co-founder, later as former co-founder now in support). The registered legal entity name was not found. The team is small, an estimated 11–50 people.

### Cited Findings
- ToS: "These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the province of Quebec, Canada"; lawsuits may only be brought in the courts of **Quebec City** (unverified snippet) — [heliumdeploy.com ToS](https://heliumdeploy.com/policies/terms-of-service); older ToS URL also exists — [heliumdeploy.com/pages/tos](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/tos)
- The ToS describes the business as "an online marketplace that allows visitors to purchase mining hardware" — [heliumdeploy.com ToS](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/tos)
- HQ is Montreal per LinkedIn — [LinkedIn company page](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/helium-deploy); Montreal with roughly 11–50 employees per ensun — [ensun.io](https://ensun.io/company/heliumdeploy-68595af722805d3154509d17-managed); Westmount, QC per ZoomInfo — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Georges-Harfouche/8531729236)

- **Emile Chouha**: "Co-Founder @ HeliumDeploy" since 2021 (aggregator) — [RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/emile-chouha-email_56695081); company profile — [RocketReach HeliumDeploy](https://rocketreach.co/heliumdeploy-profile_b7034d28c4f875ed)

### Inferences
- The legal entity is most likely a Quebec numbered company (e.g., "9xxx-xxxx Québec inc.") or a federal corporation operating under the "HeliumDeploy" trade name. The Austin, TX listing may be a stale or US-facing entity record. Before signing anything, ask for the exact contracting entity, its NEQ (Quebec enterprise number) and its EU establishment, if any.
- The company appears founder-led and small (11–50). A partnership decision likely sits with 1–2 founders. That is consistent with the manufacturers page saying submissions go "straight to the team that decides what we launch".
- Confusion risk: a different "Helium Deploy" appears in SDxCentral coverage of small-cell deployment with Baicells. This is probably the same company's 2022 Helium 5G push (see §3), but verify before quoting.

### Gaps
- The registered legal name, NEQ / business number, and any EU subsidiary or EU VAT ID were not found, because the ToS text was not readable in full.
- No current executive list (CEO title, ops/partnerships lead) was found, and no official "team" page surfaced.
- No funding information was found. Crunchbase shows no rounds in the snippets.

## 2. Scale claims, EU warehouse, and EU fulfilment (VAT/IOSS, duties)

### Takeaway
HeliumDeploy claims 57,000+ miners deployed in 160+ countries, and tells manufacturers it reaches 30,000 hardware-buying customers (self-claims, undated). Its shipping policy (dated 02-05-2026) says it ships locally from regional warehouses in Canada, the US and Europe. A snippet from its RMA page names a **Berlin** warehouse serving the UK, EU and the Nordics. Whether EU orders are delivered duties paid (DDP) is unclear, and at least one customer disputes it.

### Cited Findings
- Self-claim: "deployed over 57,000 miners in 160+ countries, enabling 278 million data packets, and customers have earned over 500 million tokens" (undated brand profile) — [Brandfetch heliumdeploy.io](https://brandfetch.com/heliumdeploy.io)
- Self-claim on the manufacturers page: access to "30,000 customers who buy hardware" — [For Manufacturers](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/manufacturers)
- Self-claim in the FAQ: a top distributor of Helium hotspots during the network's fastest growth; "we partnered in deploying $9.5M worth of Helium 5G equipment internationally" — [heliumdeploy.com (via search summary)](https://heliumdeploy.com/)
- Shipping policy: local shipping runs from regional warehouses in **Canada, US and Europe**. Shipments to other destinations may carry duties and taxes "built into the final price". Handling is **2% of order value** plus an **$8.15 signature-confirmation fee**. The policy uses **FOB Destination** terms (the seller bears risk until delivery) and is dated **02-05-2026** — [Shipping policy](https://heliumdeploy.com/policies/shipping-policy)
- RMA page: a **Berlin warehouse** serves the UK, EU and Nordics, with a 5–7 business-day returns SLA (unverified snippet) — [Free return shipping & RMA — how it works](https://heliumdeploy.com/blogs/mining-news/free-return-shipping-rma-how-it-works)
- Product pages (e.g., Miner Duster, Power Kit) mention three warehouses including Europe, but also say "additional shipping and import fees may apply" — [Miner Duster](https://heliumdeploy.com/products/miner-duster); [Power Kit](https://heliumdeploy.com/products/power-kit)
- A Trustpilot reviewer quotes the site's claim of "EU warehouses, duties handled" and disputes it, saying their order was drop-shipped from China. Another reviewer calls the company a "glorified drop shipper" (single, unverified customer accounts) — [Trustpilot heliumdeploy.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/heliumdeploy.com); [Trustpilot p.2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/heliumdeploy.com?page=2)
- A third-party review also describes HeliumDeploy as operating "three warehouses in the USA, Canada, and EU" — [ManuallyReviewed (via search summary)](https://manuallyreviewed.com/heliumdeploy-review/)
- A shipment-status page exists for order tracking — [Shipment Status](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/shipment-status)

### Inferences
- If a Berlin warehouse exists, HeliumDeploy probably holds German VAT registration and uses the EU One-Stop Shop (OSS) for B2C intra-EU distance sales. Goods stocked in the EU would not use IOSS, which only covers imports of €150 or less. Neither point was confirmed. For a Wellbian device priced above €150, IOSS would not apply anyway. The real question is who acts as **importer of record** into the EU and who holds the **CE/RED technical file**. This matters for Wellbian because the importer bears obligations under the RED and GPSR.
- The "drop-shipped from China" complaints suggest some SKUs ship directly from manufacturers rather than from the Berlin stock. A Wellbian proposal should clarify whether HeliumDeploy would hold EU inventory (with Wellbian or KWeather shipping in bulk) or expect drop-ship.

### Gaps
- No warehouse address or 3PL partner name in Berlin was found, and no EU VAT number.
- No explicit IOSS/OSS or DDP/DAP statement for EU customers was readable.
- No dated, independently verified customer count was found. The 57k/160+ figures are undated self-claims.

## 3. Brand portfolio, onboarding of new brands, recent additions, and launch/exclusive deals

### Takeaway
HeliumDeploy carries 7 DePIN brands: WeatherXM, Wingbits, 4DSKY, Anyone, GEODNET, Helium and ROVR. It also sells many ASIC/home-miner brands. It has a public **"For Manufacturers"** page with low-friction terms: a spec sheet plus a board photo, a reply within a week, no listing fee, no exclusivity, no revenue share on other channels, and a roughly 6-week, 5-step launch path that includes burn-in testing. This is the most direct entry point for Wellbian. No 2025–2026 "official launch distributor" announcement was found. The closest evidence is the HeliumDeploy-run 4DSKY limited drop and a December 2024 XProtocol partnership.

### Cited Findings
- The "All DePIN Hardware Manufacturers" page lists **WeatherXM, Wingbits, 4DSKY, Anyone, GEODNET, Helium, ROVR**. It also lists ASIC brands Bitaxe, Canaan (Avalon), Fluminer, Goldshell, Hammer Miner and Lucky Miner — [All DePIN Hardware](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/all-depin-hardware); DePIN collection: [Weather Stations, GPS, Flight Trackers](https://heliumdeploy.com/collections/depin)

### Inferences
- **Best entry path for Wellbian:** submit through /pages/manufacturers with a spec sheet, a board photo, unit economics and a certification status summary. Expect a one-week yes/no reply and roughly 6 weeks to listing. The "pressure test the numbers" step means HeliumDeploy will scrutinize reward and ROI economics, so Wellbian should prepare a sober unit-economics sheet that complies with its own rules on reward statements.
- The non-exclusive, no-fee model means HeliumDeploy can be added alongside an EU distributor (e.g., Crypship) without channel conflict, and Wellbian keeps the customer relationship.
- The "drop" format (limited units, bonus points) is a launch template HeliumDeploy already runs. Wellbian could propose a limited EU/NA drop, but must check bonus or point mechanics against Wellbian's token/airdrop statement rules.
- All current DePIN categories are outdoor or wireless (weather, GNSS, ADS-B, privacy routers, Wi-Fi). An **indoor air-quality** device would be a new category for them, which is both a differentiation argument and a risk, since the buyer audience is reward-driven.

### Gaps
- Steps 3–5 of the manufacturer process, and whether HeliumDeploy buys inventory outright (wholesale) or consigns, were not readable.
- No dated list of brand additions in 2025–2026 was found (e.g., when ROVR, Anyone or Quakecore were added).
- WeatherXM's own official reseller list was not checked in this pass; another researcher covers WeatherXM.
- No evidence of exclusive launch-distributor status for any DePIN project was found.

## 5. How token rewards and ROI are presented (regulatory sensitivity)

### Takeaway
HeliumDeploy's messaging is mixed. Its FAQ disclaims guarantees ("cannot guarantee exact results"), and a blog post ("Why ROI doesn't matter") explicitly warns about US securities-law risk in ROI claims. Yet the FAQ also cites "6–9 month" paybacks, and newsletters give per-device earnings and payback estimates (e.g., WeatherXM about $0.20/day, about 5.8-year ROI). The marketing also leads with "customers have earned over 500 million tokens". No dedicated ROI-calculator disclaimer text was found.

### Cited Findings
- FAQ: "We guarantee we'll give you the best possible chance at profit, but we cannot guarantee exact results. Crypto-based earnings have variables like token price and network usage that are beyond our control." Also: "Some miners can earn back their cost in around 6-9 months, others might take a year or more." (unverified snippet) — [heliumdeploy.com (via search summary)](https://heliumdeploy.com/)
- Blog "Why ROI doesn't matter" includes: "This also falls under some securities laws in the USA so do not touch anything of this sort." — [heliumdeploy.io blog](https://www.heliumdeploy.io/blog/why-roi-doesnt-matter)
- A newsletter estimated WeatherXM at about **$0.20/day per station**, split 75% to NFT holders and 25% to the deployer, for a payback of about **5.8 years** at then-current rates (date unknown) — [WeatherXM collection / blog via search summary](https://heliumdeploy.com/collections/depin)
- The blog features network reward estimators, e.g., the Wingbits reward estimator — [DePIN News](https://heliumdeploy.com/blogs/mining-news/depin-news-helium-abandons-discord-new-hivemapper-device-and-wingbits-reward-estimator)
- The brand claims "customers have earned over 500 million tokens" — [Brandfetch](https://brandfetch.com/heliumdeploy.io)
- Context: the SEC's January 2025 complaint against Nova Labs (Helium) concerned hotspot sales as securities. It did **not** involve HeliumDeploy — [Wikipedia: Helium Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helium_Network)
- A 2026 sector piece argues hardware depreciation is outpacing DePIN earnings — [DePIN Dispatch, 2026-10-07](https://github.com/Ricosworks1/depin-research-reports/releases/tag/depin-dispatch-hardware-depreciation-outpaces-earnings-20261007)

### Inferences
- HeliumDeploy will likely want earnings figures for a product page, since its "pressure test the numbers" step implies this. Wellbian should give it a **pre-approved statement pack** with no payback periods, no $/day, no token-price figures, and "rewards not guaranteed, may change" language, and should contractually require that HeliumDeploy not publish ROI or payback estimates for Wellbian devices. This is especially important in the EU, where MiCA and consumer-protection or unfair-commercial-practice rules apply. Wellbian's own rules are in `depin/intel/playbook.md`.
- The "Why ROI doesn't matter" post shows HeliumDeploy understands the securities angle, which is a basis for a compliance-friendly conversation.

### Gaps
- The text of any on-site ROI calculator and its footer disclaimer could not be retrieved.
- The current (2026) wording of the FAQ was not confirmed, and the snippet's indexing date is unknown.

## 7. Compliance documentation (CE/FCC/IC, RED cyber) and regulatory statements

### Takeaway
No evidence was found that HeliumDeploy publishes or requires CE, FCC, IC or RED (including the RED delegated-act cybersecurity rules that apply from August 2025) documentation from manufacturers. Its manufacturer process emphasises "numbers" and "burn-in" testing rather than certification. Wellbian should bring its own compliance file and clarify who is the EU importer of record.

### Cited Findings
- No HeliumDeploy-specific certification record or compliance page surfaced in searches. Results returned only Helium's own guidance that the **manufacturer** must obtain CE-RED (EU868) and FCC certification per country — [Helium docs: CE RED compliance](https://docs.helium.com/hotspot-makers/ce-red-compliance); [Helium docs: FCC compliance](https://docs.helium.com/hotspot-makers/fcc-compliance)
- The manufacturer process mentions burn-in testing ("every board runs before a single one ships") but no certification step in the visible snippet — [For Manufacturers](https://heliumdeploy.com/pages/manufacturers)
- Product pages note "additional shipping and import fees may apply", but no conformity statements were visible in the snippets — [Power Kit](https://heliumdeploy.com/products/power-kit)

### Inferences
- If HeliumDeploy's Berlin stock is imported by HeliumDeploy, it becomes the EU **importer**. Under the RED (2014/53/EU) and the GPSR (EU 2023/988) it must then hold the DoC, a name and address on the product or packaging, and access to the technical file. Wellbian should offer a full pack: EU DoC, RED test reports (including EN 18031 for RED Art. 3.3(d/e/f) cybersecurity if the device has radio and internet connectivity), RoHS, WEEE registration, GPSR responsible-person details, and user documentation in the local language.
- The absence of a stated compliance gate is a lower barrier to listing, but it puts the regulatory burden on Wellbian. A partner that does not ask may also not handle market-surveillance enquiries well.

### Gaps
- Whether HeliumDeploy requests the DoC or certificates at onboarding is unknown. Ask directly.
- No public statement on EU WEEE, battery or packaging EPR registration by HeliumDeploy was found.
