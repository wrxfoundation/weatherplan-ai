## 2. Catalog today: brands, device types, weather/air-quality sensors, prices, own services

### Takeaway
Hexaspot is a LoRaWAN- and Helium-centric IoT shop and a RAKwireless Gold partner. It sells WeatherXM WS2000, GEODNET, DIMO, Hivemapper and Meshtastic hardware plus LoRa sensors from Dragino, Browan, Digital Matter and Trackpac. FreshMiners is a multi-project DePIN shop: GEODNET (including the "GEODNET Weather Station"), Wingbits, DIMO, Hivemapper, ROVR, YOM, NATIX, Anyone, Aethir, Sourceful and others. **Neither showed an indoor air-quality (CO2/PM) product in search results**, so Wellbian would be a new category for both.

## 3. How new brands get listed (partner/wholesale pages, requirements, dropship vs stock, history)

### Takeaway

Partnerships appear to be arranged through bilateral reseller or distributor agreements started by the DePIN project (DIMO ARP, Wingbits Approved Distributors, WeatherXM reseller list). FreshMiners has a documented record of being the **first international DIMO reseller (Mar 2023)**.

### Cited Findings
- No "list your project", manufacturer-application or partner-requirements page was found on freshminers.com. — search result summary for [FreshMiners Brands](https://freshminers.com/brands/)
- DIMO announced FreshMiners as its first international Authorized Reseller on 7 Mar 2023 and pointed NL and BE buyers to FreshMiners. The announcement quotes DIMO COO Alex Rawitz and FreshMiners MD Wilko van Dorresteijn. — [DIMO news](https://dimo.org/news/dimo-authorized-reseller-freshminers); [DePINHub](https://depinhub.io/news/dimo-welcomes-fresh-miners-as-first-international-authorized-reseller-14344); [DIMO LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dimo-network_dimo-authorized-reseller-freshminers-activity-7039603587483586560-oDSs)

A distributor is a reseller who can store inventory and fulfil orders. — [DIMO ARP docs](https://docs.dimo.org/dimo-ecosystem/distributors/become-an-authorized-reseller-program-arp-member); [Reseller vs distributor](https://docs.dimo.org/dimo-ecosystem/distributors/reseller-vs-distributor)
- Wingbits lists FreshMiners as an approved distributor for Europe (WB200, MGW310). From 14 Oct 2024, all new devices on the network must be "Wingbits Approved Hardware". — [Wingbits approved distributors](https://docs.wingbits.com/project/wingbits-approved-distributors)
- WeatherXM's reseller page lists Hexaspot among approved stores, alongside Dailyminers and regional groups, and invites shops to apply. The snapshot was about 509 days old, and it did not state an NL-exclusive territory. — [WeatherXM Resellers](https://weatherxm.com/resellers/)
- Hivemapper keeps an "Authorized Resellers" docs page. Whether either shop is listed was not verified. — [Hivemapper Authorized Resellers](https://docs.hivemapper.com/resources/authorized-resellers)
- Hexaspot's own product pages say devices come "from authorised dealers". — [Hexaspot UK shipping blog](https://hexaspot.com/blogs/news/hexaspot-now-ships-to-the-uk-meshtastic-meshcore-devices-available)

### Inferences
- The DePIN projects seem to drive onboarding through their authorized-reseller programs. Wellbian would therefore need its own reseller kit (terms, margin, MOQ, marketing assets, onboarding docs) and would approach each shop's owner directly.
- Both shops appear to hold stock and ship from NL. Hexaspot advertises same-day dispatch and FreshMiners holds an EORI number. The DIMO "distributor = holds inventory" model suggests FreshMiners is at least able to stock goods. Dropship arrangements were not found.

### Gaps
- Margins, MOQs, exclusivity terms and wholesale pricing were not found for either shop.

## 8. CE/RED compliance documentation and MiCA mentions

### Takeaway
No public statement was found from either shop about the CE/RED/RoHS documents they require from brands, and neither mentions MiCA. Hexaspot's "authorised dealers + manufacturer warranty" language and its manuals library point to a document-heavy onboarding, but this is not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Hexaspot says devices come from authorised dealers and carry manufacturer warranties, and it keeps a public manuals page. — [UK shipping blog](https://hexaspot.com/blogs/news/hexaspot-now-ships-to-the-uk-meshtastic-meshcore-devices-available); [Manuals](https://hexaspot.com/pages/manuals)
- Hexaspot's Meshtastic kits are pre-configured for EU868, which shows awareness of radio-band compliance. — [Meshtastic Kits](https://hexaspot.com/collections/meshtastic-kits-bundles)
- No CE declaration, RED, WEEE or MiCA text from either shop was found. — search result summary, no source page

### Inferences
- Any EU distributor selling a radio device (Wi-Fi, LoRa or BLE) is legally the "importer" or "distributor" under the RED and GPSR rules. Wellbian should expect to supply a CE DoC, a RED test report, a RoHS statement, WEEE registration or arrangement, and an EU responsible person, whether or not the shop asks for them up front. This is a general regulatory inference, not shop-specific.

### Gaps
- Each shop's actual vendor-onboarding checklist is unknown and has to be asked for directly. Neither shop's stance on token/MiCA marketing was found.
