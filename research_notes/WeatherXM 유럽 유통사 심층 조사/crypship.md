# Crypship.io: profile of a German multi-brand DePIN hardware shop, for a Wellbian Labs EU reseller pitch (as of 2026-10-10)

> **How this was researched.** The proxy blocked direct fetches of crypship.io, trustpilot.com, openregister.de, docs.wingbits.com and web.archive.org (DNS failure or 403). Every finding below comes from **search-engine result summaries** of the cited URLs, not from reading the pages. Treat each one as **unverified, from a search summary**. The search tool also reported page ages: several Crypship pages were indexed about 340–470 days ago, so prices and terms may have changed. Personal emails and phone numbers are deliberately left out.

## 1. Legal entity, address, founding, people, Impressum

### Takeaway
Crypship is a trade name of **Kansi Solutions GmbH**. The company is registered in **Namborn (Saarland), Germany**, at the Saarbrücken district court (Amtsgericht) under HRB 107712, with VAT ID DE346326134. It was founded on 26 Aug 2021 as a UG and later converted to a GmbH with €25,000 share capital. The managing directors are Jonas Becker and Konstantin Nidens, and the team is small (1–10 people). The same GmbH also runs **RTKdata.com**, a commercial RTK correction-data service built on GNSS/GEODNET-type infrastructure. Crypship is therefore one brand of a small "Kansi Group" that sells DePIN hardware and also operates and monetises DePIN infrastructure.

## 3. How new brands get listed (partner, B2B, wholesale, dropship, terms)

### Takeaway
No public "list your hardware" or brand-partner page was found. Crypship gets brands by becoming an **official or certified distributor** on each protocol's approved-vendor list (Wingbits, GEODNET/HYFIX). On the buyer side it runs a **Bulk Orders page for fleets and resellers**, with exclusive pricing and dedicated support. A new brand like Wellbian would therefore need direct outreach to the managing directors. The most likely model is that Crypship holds stock, since it markets "ships from Germany."

### Cited Findings
- The **Bulk Orders** page "makes bulk orders easy for fleets and resellers," offering exclusive pricing and dedicated support. The request form asks for product, quantity, customizations, order frequency, a contact number and special instructions. — [Bulk Orders](https://crypship.io/bulk-orders/)
- **Wingbits** lists Crypship as an approved or authorised distributor of WB200 and MGW310 hardware. The same page notes that from 14 Oct 2024 only Wingbits Approved Hardware can join the network. — [Wingbits approved distributors](https://docs.wingbits.com/project/wingbits-approved-distributors)
- **GEODNET** lists Crypship.io as a certified base-station vendor alongside Depinsa, HYFIX India, Novyx.Tech, Village Island, Paradox Wireless and IDeploy. **HYFIX's** distributor list also includes Crypship.io next to Rock Robotic, Freshminers, Hepta Networks and Helium Deploy. — [GEODNET certified vendors](https://docs.geodnet.com/geod-mining/certified-geodnet-base-station-vendors); [HYFIX GEODNET devices](https://hyfix.ai/collections/frontpage); [GEODNET products](https://geodnet.com/products)
- **WeatherXM's** official reseller page lists Hexaspot (EU/UK), Cosmic Equip and Dailyminers (EU), EuGeo (GR/CY/EU), Eurosupplies and TechnoStoreX. **Crypship does not appear**, though the page snapshot was indexed about 509 days ago. — [WeatherXM Resellers](https://weatherxm.com/resellers/)
- The catalog has grown beyond Helium and DIMO to SkyX, ROVR, Soarchain and Nubila. The search summaries do not date when each brand was added. — [About Us](https://crypship.io/about-us/); [SkyX SKY-100](https://crypship.io/product/sky-100/)

### Inferences
- The way to get onto Crypship's shelf is to be a protocol with an **approved-hardware or certified-vendor framework**. Wellbian should arrive with a ready **"Wellbian Certified Reseller" structure**: an authorised-vendor list on the Wellbian site, a reseller agreement, and device onboarding that only works through approved channels. That mirrors how Wingbits and GEODNET work with Crypship.
- Wingbits' approved-hardware rule took effect on 14 Oct 2024, and Crypship was on the list. This suggests Crypship moves quickly to get listed when a protocol formalises a vendor programme.
- Because Crypship serves fleets and resellers in bulk, it could act as an **EU sub-distributor** for Wellbian, not only a retail shelf.

### Gaps
- No public information on dropship versus stocked models, MOQs, payment terms (prepay or net), margins or exclusivity.
- No public onboarding requirements for new brands, such as CE/RED certificates, WEEE/battery registration or token-compliance documentation.
- How fast past brands were added, with dated examples, is unknown.

## 4. Shipping, VAT/IOSS, returns and warranty, support channels

### Takeaway
Crypship ships from Germany, emails a tracking link and an invoice once the order is paid, promises a 24-hour reply, and offers a "Best-Price Guarantee." Support runs by email through a generic support mailbox on the crypship.io domain and a Support Center page. No specific evidence was found on the countries served, IOSS or customs handling, the return or withdrawal period, or warranty length.

### Cited Findings
- The homepage advertises "fast shipping, Best-Price Guarantee, 24h response." — [Crypship homepage](https://crypship.io/)
- The Support Center says the tracking link is emailed once the item reaches the shipping partner, the invoice is emailed after payment, and support "will check availability and possible discounts." Support is reached through a generic support@ address on the crypship.io domain. — [Support Center](https://crypship.io/support-center/)
- A launch-style discount code, **CRYPSHIP5** (5% off any product), appeared on the shop page about a year ago. — [Shop](https://crypship.io/shop/)
- Searches for Crypship's terms, withdrawal right, warranty, customs and IOSS returned **no Crypship-specific results**. — [search via ScamAdviser page only](https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website/crypship.io)

### Inferences
- As a German B2C seller, Crypship is legally bound by the 14-day EU right of withdrawal and the 2-year statutory warranty (Gewährleistung). These are legal defaults, not policies confirmed on its site.
- Shipping from Germany means VAT is handled under intra-EU OSS. Whether Crypship uses IOSS or DDP for non-EU destinations such as the UK, CH or the US is unknown.

### Gaps
- Countries served, carriers, the location of an EU warehouse beyond "ships from Germany," IOSS/DDP handling, return-shipping cost and warranty terms all need checking on the live site or by asking directly.
- No Discord or Telegram support channel was found.

## 5. Community and marketing: Discord, X, LinkedIn, content, affiliate, reward disclaimers

### Takeaway
Crypship runs a **tiered affiliate program** (Level 1 "Coin Collector": 5% commission for 0–4 referrals). No official X, Discord, Telegram or LinkedIn accounts turned up in searches, so the community footprint looks thin. Product copy describes rewards as "earn weekly rewards," and the search summaries did not include any risk disclaimer.

### Cited Findings
- Affiliate page: a tiered program, with Level 1 "Coin Collector" paying a 5% commission for 0–4 referrals. The page was about 471 days old when indexed. — [Affiliate – Crypship](https://crypship.io/affiliate/)
- Searches for Crypship on X, Twitter or LinkedIn returned **no official accounts**, only shop pages, review sites and partner docs. — [seositecheckup report](https://seositecheckup.com/seo-audit/crypship.io); [Wingbits distributors](https://docs.wingbits.com/project/wingbits-approved-distributors)
- Reward wording on product pages: DIMO AutoPi "helps you earn weekly $DIMO rewards when you stay connected and drive at least once per week," and SkyX SKY-100 "can earn weekly rewards when you share your weather data." — [DIMO AutoPi](https://crypship.io/product/dimo-autopi/); [SkyX SKY-100](https://crypship.io/product/sky-100/)

### Inferences
- Crypship is a **storefront and SEO-led** seller, not a community-led one. It relies on protocols' vendor lists and its affiliates for traffic. Wellbian would have to bring the demand: listing Crypship on Wellbian's official reseller page, plus co-marketing. Crypship is unlikely to build an audience for a new brand on its own.
- The "earn weekly rewards" copy is promotional. Under Wellbian's playbook, which bans yield promises, any co-listing copy should be supplied by Wellbian with an approved disclaimer, not left to the shop.

### Gaps
- No setup guides, tutorials or blog content from Crypship were confirmed. Competitor HeliumDeploy runs a blog; Crypship's equivalent was not found.
- Current affiliate tiers above Level 1 are unknown.
- Whether the site carries any general token-reward disclaimer page is unknown.

## 7. MiCA, regulatory statements, compliance documents required from projects

### Takeaway
**No public statement** by Crypship or Kansi on MiCA, token compliance or the documents it requires from DePIN projects was found. The closest compliance signal is indirect: Crypship lists only "protocol-approved devices" and appears on official vendor lists. Its sister brand RTKdata uses formal B2B Terms of Service.

### Cited Findings
- Crypship positions itself on "protocol-approved devices." — [Bulk Orders](https://crypship.io/bulk-orders/)
- RTKdata, the same GmbH, publishes Terms of Service, a Privacy Policy and an Imprint, and contracts B2B as Kansi Solutions GmbH. — [RTKdata docs – Contracts & Legal](https://docs.rtkdata.com/commercial/contracts-and-legal); [RTKdata Terms](https://rtkdata.com/terms/)

### Inferences
- Crypship sells hardware, not tokens, so MiCA's direct burden sits mostly with the token issuer. Crypship's main exposure is consumer-protection and advertising rules (reward claims) and product compliance (CE/RED, WEEE, battery registration). Wellbian should arrive with a **compliance pack**:
  - CE/RED declaration of conformity for the device
  - WEEE/EAR (Stiftung EAR) and battery-law registration status, or a statement that the importer of record carries them
  - a MiCA posture memo, such as a white paper status or a "no token sale via reseller" clause
  - approved marketing language with a reward disclaimer
- Because Kansi has run formal B2B contracts through RTKdata, it will probably expect a written reseller agreement rather than an informal deal.

### Gaps
- Crypship's actual onboarding checklist for new brands and any MiCA-related policy are unknown. Both need to be asked directly.

### Pitch-relevant summary (inference, for the report writer)
1. **Who decides:** a two-director GmbH (Becker and Nidens) in Saarland. Expect short decision cycles and no partnership team, so approach the founders directly through the company channel. They already run a B2B data business (RTKdata), which makes them a credible audience for "hardware plus data" partnerships.
2. **Category gap:** the catalog has weather (SkyX, Nubila), GNSS (GEODNET), ADS-B (Wingbits), mobility (DIMO, ROVR, Soarchain) and IoT (Helium), but **no air-quality or indoor-environment DePIN**. Wellbian would add a new category.
3. **How they list brands:** through official or certified-vendor frameworks. Offer Crypship "Wellbian Authorized EU Distributor" status on Wellbian's reseller page, with an approved-hardware onboarding gate, as Wingbits and GEODNET do.
4. **Fit to their model:** they already serve "fleets and resellers" in bulk, so propose an EU sub-distributor model with bulk tiers, not only a retail listing.
5. **What to bring:** CE/RED documents, WEEE/battery registration plan, DE/EN product copy with reward disclaimers, a MiCA posture note, a draft reseller agreement, MOQ and pricing tiers, and an EU-warehouse or stock plan.
