A phase opens only when both the device count and the revenue condition are met. If revenue falls short, the next phase stays closed and emission may be cut.

When the phase changes, devices already installed receive the same amount. Joining earlier means a larger cumulative quantity.

Genesis device = bought in the first sale, +20%. The top 10% by data quality earn more; 80–95% transmission is reduced, and below 80% earns nothing.

Rewards are defined only as quantities. Neither the amount paid nor its value is guaranteed, and this table is not a forecast of returns.
