We work with KWeather, a Korean weather company with 30 years of observation, and run a weather data market: city-level weather for 172 cities in 91 countries, each value a consensus across eleven weather feeds, with every input fingerprinted to a public ledger, and a verdict of met, not met or withheld.

Settlement-grade weather data has to be verifiable, location-indexed, standardised and on time. A single station fails that test exactly when it matters: big storms are when single stations most often go offline or miss the peak, and official post-event reports can take months. Our approach is to publish one consensus value with its full input record, and to withhold rather than guess when the inputs disagree.

We are in active discussions with prediction-market platforms about launching weather markets, and we think the same data could serve parametric and risk-transfer work. Would you be open to a 20-minute call so I can hear where the gaps are on your side?

Best regards,
Logan (Seowoo Park)
Head of Partnerships & Operations, Wellbian Labs
