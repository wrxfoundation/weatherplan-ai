## 경력 설명 (1995/2,000자)
```
KWeather / wellbian
Building a weather measurement network out of instruments people own and run - indoors today, extending to outdoor, radar and satellite.

Weather data has always been produced by a handful of institutions and consumed by everyone else. wellbian inverts that. A certified sensor sits in a home or on a building, measures continuously, and every reading is fingerprinted to the XRP Ledger - so the data can be verified by whoever uses it rather than taken on trust. Alongside the device network, we publish city-level weather for 172 cities in 91 countries, each value a consensus of eleven weather feeds. KWeather, our device partner, has measured Korea's weather for 30 years and supplies data to 4,000+ enterprise clients; wellbian runs the network.

The same data runs through our Weather AI Factory, which turns it into a weather AI agent, refined datasets and data and inference APIs, with access through a pass issued on the XRP Ledger.

The ledger entry is the proof, not the product. What the network is built to supply is the verified layer itself - readings aggregated so no single home is identifiable, and checkable against the record before anyone relies on them.
```
