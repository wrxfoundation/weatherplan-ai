What we make

A certified indoor air monitor that people run in their own homes, and a ledger record for what it measures.

The device

Weather Data Token Generator™ (ARC-600DA). Measures CO₂, PM2.5/PM10, TVOC, temperature and humidity. Built by KWeather, KC-certified, wall or desk.

The ledger

Each device carries a device NFT (XLS-20). Readings are fingerprinted and anchored on the XRP Ledger; raw data stays off-chain. Devices are priced in RLUSD and settled on XRPL.

The data economy

Verified, de-identified data becomes weather services and datasets for enterprise buyers, with machine-to-machine pricing (x402). Rewards to device owners are planned and in testing. Amounts and value are not guaranteed.

Our published token policy
